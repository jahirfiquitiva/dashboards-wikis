{{Lib}} is a dashboard for icon packs. It gives you a complete Android app, ready to fill with your own icons, and it also supports icon requests, wallpapers and Kustom assets.
<!--/only-->

You don't need to be an Android developer to use it. Most of the setup is editing a few files and copying your images in. This wiki walks you through every step.

## How to use this wiki

The sidebar groups every page by what it's for:

1. **Start here**: get the project running, and set your package name. Do this first.
2. **Basic setup**: your app's name, icon, colors and main settings. Go through these pages in order.
<!--only:blueprint-->
3. **Icon pack**: add your icons and set up icon requests.
4. **Wallpapers** and **Kustom**: only if your app includes them.
5. **Optional**: extras like notifications and donations.
6. **Maintenance**: keep your app up to date.
